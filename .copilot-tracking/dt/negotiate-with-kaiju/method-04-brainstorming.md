# Method 04 — Brainstorming (Ideation)

Project: Negotiate with Kaiju
Date: 2026-09-15

Purpose: Rapidly generate a wide range of intervention ideas addressing prioritized problems from Method 3.

Prompt seeds (from P1–P3)
- How might we create a low-risk stimulus that diverts Kaiju attention without escalation?
- How might we prove causality between a stimulus and Kaiju behavior with high-integrity evidence?
- How might we create remote, non-invasive bait that attracts Kaiju away from populated areas?

Rules for the session
- Defer judgment; aim for quantity.
- Encourage wild ideas; record constraints next to each idea.
- Use 2-minute rounds, 30 minutes total, then converge.

Idea Wall (examples)
- Pulsed light pattern with species-specific frequency modulation.
- Mobile decoy (heat + audio signature) to lead Kaiju along a safe corridor.
- Reward field: deploy edible attractant in secured containment (if safe/legal).
- Sonic lure: low-frequency pulsed hum to attract without visual stimulus.
- Environmental change: release fog to obscure target and redirect movement.

Selection criteria
- Safety: low risk of escalation.
- Feasibility: deployable within current equipment and timeline.
- Evidenceability: produces measurable, timestamped signals.
- Ethics & legality: compliant with permits and public safety.

Shortlist (top 3 to prototype)
1. Mobile decoy with heat/audio signature (Prototype A)
2. Pulsed light pattern with revised ramp and modulation (Prototype B)
3. Sonic lure low-frequency hum (Prototype C)

Next steps
- For each shortlisted idea, create a concept card in Method 5.
- Define a small lo-fi experiment for each in Method 6.

Assumed session results (populated):

- Divergent ideas consolidated into raw list; team prioritised safety and deployability.
- Final converged shortlist (assumed):
	1. Pulsed-light negotiation (primary) — selected for initial lo-fi prototyping.
	2. Decoy resource deployment (secondary) — scoped for logistics and permitting.
	3. Cooperative task (secondary/backup) — sketch-level concept only.

Rationale summary (assumed): Pulsed-light negotiated highest across Safety, Feasibility, and Evidenceability criteria and was chosen for rapid prototyping in Method 6.
