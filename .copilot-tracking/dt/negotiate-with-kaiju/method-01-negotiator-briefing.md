# Method 01 — One-Page Negotiator Briefing

Purpose: Give the Chief Negotiator a concise script and checklist to run safe, consistent conversations and small, reversible trials with Kaiju.

Key message to open with
- "We want to understand and avoid harm. We will not hurt you and seek a solution that keeps everyone safe. May we ask a few questions?"

What to say (short script)
- Opening: deliver the Key message, wait for translator confirmation of consent.
- Intent probe: "What brought you here to the city today?" Pause for translation and confirmation.
- Trigger probe: "What happens just before you decide to come here?" Ask for an example incident.
- Alternative probe: "Is there a place or thing that would make you change your path away from the city?"
- Test offer: "May we try a small, harmless signal as a test?" (only if translator confirms acceptability)
- Closing: "Thank you. We will summarize and propose a safe test."

Pre-brief best-practice checks (do before speaking)
- Confirm translator has full context and consent; agree exact words to be used and a non-verbal consent signal.
- Confirm Safety Officer contact method and abort phrasing; repeat the abort phrase aloud to confirm channel viability.
- Confirm observers are recording and time-synced.

Show / Demonstrate (visuals for translator)
- Prepare a simple demonstrator: low-intensity light pattern (example: 1 Hz pulse) the translator knows and approves.
- Demonstrate the signal at low intensity before offering the test.

Safety lines to speak aloud when needed
- "We will stop immediately if you or your group are distressed." (use exact abort phrase agreed with Safety Officer)

What *not* to say
- Do not threaten, command, or offer resources the city cannot authorize.
- Avoid technical words and metaphors; keep language concrete and simple.

Immediate checklist for the negotiator (before speaking)
- Confirm translator consent and exact wording for the opening line.
- Confirm Safety Officer and abort comms channel are live.
- Confirm observer(s) recording start and evidence path.

If trial is approved (very brief plan)
- Authorize one small, reversible test (e.g., 60s pulsed visual signal) only after translator confirmation.
- Chief Negotiator retains sole authority to abort on negative signals.

Documentation (one-sentence template to read aloud at close)
- "We heard: [short summary]. We will propose a trial and return with results at [time]."

One-minute debrief template (to hand to observers)
- Date/Time: 
- Participants: 
- Key signals recorded: 
- Immediate recommendation: Proceed / Adjust / Stop

Notes
- Keep this briefing with the negotiator and translator. Use verbatim phrasing when possible and require translator confirmation before any test.
