# Method 01 — Negotiation Protocol (Chief Negotiator + Kaiju Translator)

Purpose: A compact, safety-first protocol to guide the Chief Negotiator and Kaiju Translator during live engagements to discover motives, triggers, and acceptable alternatives while preserving evidence.

Roles
- Chief Negotiator: leads the dialogue, enforces safety stop rules, summarizes and confirms findings.
- Kaiju Translator: interprets signals, confirms meaning, and advises on culturally-appropriate probes.

Guiding Principles
- Safety first: follow stop rules immediately on escalation.
- One question / probe at a time; allow translation and confirmation.
- Neutral language: avoid leading or threatening phrasing.
- Capture verbatim signals and context (time, location, environment).
- Prefer small, reversible trials to validate hypotheses.

Script outline
1) Opening (1–3 min)
- Statement: “We seek to understand and avoid harm. May we ask a few questions?”
- Translator confirms consent and notes non-verbal cues.

2) Core intent probes
- "Why are you coming to the city?"
- "What do you need or seek here?"
- Ask for examples and recent incidents.

3) Trigger probes
- "What happens just before you decide to come here?"
- "Are there sounds, lights, smells, machines, or structures that draw or upset you?"

4) Acceptable alternatives
- "What would make you shift your attention away from the city?"
- "Is there a place, object, or event that would satisfy this need?"

5) Safe trial offer (small, reversible)
- Propose a low-risk test (example: non-threatening signal, staged sound) and ask: "May we try this as a test?"
- If accepted, observe and record immediate response; stop on negative reaction.

6) Confirm and close
- Summarize observed signals and translator's interpretation.
- Agree immediate next steps and safety boundaries.

Safety stop rules (immediate pause and withdraw)
- Clear charging/aggressive approach or vocal distress
- Secondary Kaiju appear or numbers increase unexpectedly
- Translator signals ambiguity or inability to confirm meaning
- Any human participant signals unsafe conditions

Recording checklist (must capture)
- Date, time, and precise location
- Exact words/signals and translator notes (verbatim when possible)
- Non-verbal cues and environmental conditions
- Test offered, acceptance, and measured response
- People present and roles

- Key observations (3 bullets): triggers, acceptable alternatives, red lines
- Immediate recommendation: (one-line next step)

Notes
- Keep probes short and iterative.
- Do not promise outcomes or resources without city leadership approval.
- Record everything: evidence quality matters for Method 2 analysis.
