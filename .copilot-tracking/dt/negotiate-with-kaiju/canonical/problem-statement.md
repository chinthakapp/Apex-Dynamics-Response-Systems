# Problem Statement

## Problem Statement

The <insufficient knowledge> is producing a pattern of repeated destruction and threat for city residents and infrastructure. How might we identify and redirect Kaiju interest toward productive resources or zones so that we can provide reduced attacks and safer city operations?

#### Questions to Ask

- What known signals or events precede recent attacks?
- Are there seasonal, territorial, or resource-based patterns in Kaiju behavior?
- What would a minimally acceptable diversion look like to city leadership?
- Which stakeholder can validate Kaiju intent or signals?
