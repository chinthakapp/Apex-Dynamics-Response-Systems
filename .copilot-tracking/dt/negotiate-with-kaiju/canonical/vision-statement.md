# Vision Statement

## Vision Statement

Negotiate with Kaiju to transform hostile interactions into coordinated, non-destructive engagements that benefit both the city and Kaiju populations.

### Why This Matters

Kaiju attacks cause loss of life, infrastructure damage, and long-term disruption. A negotiated and redirected relationship could reduce harm, enable safer coexistence, and unlock new opportunities for productive engagement between humans and Kaiju.

#### Questions to Ask

- What outcomes would Kaiju consider "productive" or valuable?
- Who would need to approve negotiated interaction from the city's side?
- What immediate safety constraints must be enforced during engagement?
