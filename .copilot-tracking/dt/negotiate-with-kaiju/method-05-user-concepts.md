# Method 05 — User Concepts

Project: Negotiate with Kaiju
Date: 2026-09-15

Purpose: Convert shortlisted ideas into concise concept cards for quick desirability checks and feasibility feedback.

Concept Card template
- Title:
- Core idea:
- Who benefits:
- Key assumptions:
- Risks:
- How to test (lo-fi):

Concept A — Mobile Decoy
- Core idea: A remote, wheeled platform that emits heat and recorded Kaiju-preferred audio signatures to lure Kaiju along a safe corridor.
- Who benefits: city protection teams and civilians.
- Key assumptions: Kaiju respond predictably to heat+audio; decoy can withstand interaction.
- Risks: potential escalation, device damage, environmental contamination.
- How to test: small-scale activation at safe distance with observers, use heavy-duty protective casing.

Concept B — Pulsed Light Modulation
- Core idea: Refined pulsed light pattern tuned to reduce aggression while redirecting attention.
- Key assumptions: specific frequency range influences attention; no harm to humans.
- Risks: unintended attraction to crowds, wildlife impact.
- How to test: controlled pulsed sequences during dry-run with observers and evidence capture.

Concept C — Sonic Lure
- Core idea: Low-frequency hum emitted from distant speakers to attract or nudge Kaiju.
- Key assumptions: Kaiju react to specific low-frequency cues.
- Risks: loudness impacts humans, may escalate behavior.
- How to test: low-power hum tests at increasing distances while monitoring Kaiju response.

Decision (assumed):
- Selected for lo-fi prototyping: Concept B — Pulsed Light Modulation (primary) and Concept A — Mobile Decoy (secondary scoping).

Evaluation lenses applied (assumed scores):
- Desirability: Pulsed Light (High), Decoy (Medium), Sonic Lure (Low)
- Feasibility: Pulsed Light (High), Decoy (Medium), Sonic Lure (Low)
- Viability (operational): Pulsed Light (High), Decoy (Medium), Sonic Lure (Low)
