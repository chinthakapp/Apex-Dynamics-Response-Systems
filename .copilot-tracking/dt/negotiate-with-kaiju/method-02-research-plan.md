# Method 02 — Design Research Plan

Purpose
- Run lightweight, targeted research and observation to validate hypotheses about what triggers Kaiju attention and to collect evidence for negotiation strategy.

Objectives
- Identify proximate triggers (signals, resources, activities) that precede Kaiju visits.
- Verify translator interpretations of Kaiju signals and establish a shared signal lexicon.
- Test whether the pulsed-light stimulus elicits orientation or avoidance responses.
- Collect high-quality, time‑synced evidence for analysis and canonical artifacts.

Research Questions
- What measurable events or environmental conditions occur immediately before attacks?
- Which stakeholder-observed patterns correlate with Kaiju approach or withdrawal?
- How do Kaiju respond to low-risk visual signals in controlled trials?

Scope & Constraints
- Short, focused field observations and semi-structured interviews; no invasive or high-risk experiments.
- All field work follows safety stop rules and permits listed in the trial packet.

Stakeholders to Interview / Observe
- Primary: Chief Negotiator, Kaiju Translator, Safety Officer, Trial Lead (Ops)
- Secondary: City leadership (emergency command), Civil Defense, Infrastructure operators, Selected residents near perimeter (if safe)

Methods
- Semi-structured interviews (use `method-02-interview-guides.md`).
- Observational trials using the `method-02-trial-plan.md` and `method-02-observation-checklist.md`.
- Translator-led signal validation sessions (short, repeatable, recorded).

Sampling & Recruitment
- Begin with 4–6 interviews: Chief Negotiator, Translator, Safety Officer, Trial Lead, 1 City Leader, 1 Infrastructure SME.
- Recruit observers from Ops and Civil Defense to attend trials and fill checklists.

Data Capture & Handling
- Use NTP-synced cameras, microphones, GPS, and the observation checklist. Store evidence under `.copilot-tracking/dt/negotiate-with-kaiju/evidence/{trial-id}/`.
- Record interview audio and notes; timestamped transcripts to be entered into the translator annotation tool or saved as artifacts.
- Preserve SHA256 checksums for all files; maintain access controls on evidence bucket.

Analysis Plan
- Quick analysis: summarize immediate trial outcomes (success/partial/fail) and attach checklist evidence.
- Thematic analysis of interviews to extract signals, constraints, and negotiation levers.
- Produce `method-02-synthesis.md` with consolidated findings and recommended next steps for Method 3.

Deliverables
- Interview transcripts and short summaries (one page each).
- Completed observation checklists and evidence bundles per trial.
- `method-02-synthesis.md` (themes, validated triggers, recommended negotiation offers).

Timeline (recommended)
- Day 0: Prep and briefing (packet review). Dry run with Ops.
- Day 1: Conduct 4–6 interviews and one observation trial.
- Day 2: Analyze evidence, produce synthesis, and plan next trials.

Ethics & Consent
- Use the `method-02-consent-form.md` for all human participants. Do not record or share translator data without consent.
