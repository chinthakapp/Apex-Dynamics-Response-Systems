# Method 02 — Interview Guides

Purpose: Short, semi-structured guides for interviews with core stakeholders. Keep questions focused, ask for examples, and always request timestamps and context for stories.

Guide: Chief Negotiator
- Opening: "Please describe recent incidents that led to Kaiju approaches. Where and when did they occur?"
- "What patterns have you observed about timing, approach vectors, and targets?"
- "What interventions have been tried? What worked/failed?"
- "What would count as an acceptable diversion or negotiated outcome?"
- Closing: "Who else should we speak to?"

Guide: Kaiju Translator
- Opening: "How do you interpret the Kaiju signals we have recorded?"
- "Which signals reliably indicate approach vs agitation vs communication?"
- "Can you walk us through a recent event and map signals to actions?"
- Signal validation: play a short, low-risk demonstrator signal and ask translator to annotate expected meaning.

Guide: Safety Officer / Trial Lead
- "What are the operational constraints for trials? (personnel limits, safe distances, equipment)"
- "Describe abort triggers and previous near-miss events we should be aware of."
- "What logistics (vehicles, staging) do we need to run a safe trial?"

Guide: City Leadership / Infrastructure
- "What constraints (legal, political, resource) should the team be aware of?"
- "What approvals are needed for repeating trials or scale-up?"

Guide: Local Resident / Witness (if safe to interview)
- "Describe where you were and what you saw when the Kaiju appeared."
- "Did you notice anything unusual before the event (sound, light, vehicles)?"

Interview logistics
- Target duration: 20–40 minutes. Record audio with consent.
- Capture verbatim quotes and local timestamps. Note uncertainty explicitly.
