# Method 02 — Observation Checklist

Purpose: Short, practical checklist for observers during any trial diversion or negotiation test. Use to collect consistent, repeatable evidence for Method 2 analysis.

Why: Ensures data quality and comparability across trials so we can validate whether a proposed diversion reduces Kaiju attention.

Instructions: One observer fills this out during each trial. Record exact times and short, factual notes. Attach or reference any audio/video logs.
