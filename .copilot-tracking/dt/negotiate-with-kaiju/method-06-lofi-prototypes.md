# Method 06 — Low-Fidelity Prototypes

Project: Negotiate with Kaiju
Date: 2026-09-15

Purpose: Build scrappy, fast prototypes to test the riskiest assumptions with minimal cost and time.

Prototype A — Mobile Decoy (Lo-fi)
- Build: insulated box on wheeled cart, heat source (controlled), speaker with audio signature.
- Safety: remote activation, physical barrier between test corridor and observers.
- Success criteria: Kaiju approach vector changes by >30% toward decoy in at least one trial.

Prototype B — Pulsed Light Modulation (Lo-fi)
- Build: LED rig capable of programmable pulse rates and modulation.
- Safety: start at low intensity; observers stationed at secure perimeter.
- Success criteria: measurable change in Kaiju orientation or approach velocity without increased aggression.

Prototype C — Sonic Lure (Lo-fi)
- Build: low-frequency speaker array on mobile platform.
- Safety: volume limits, immediate abort controls.
- Success criteria: detectable attraction metric without escalation.

Assumed lo-fi results (populated):

- Pulsed Light Modulation (Prototype B) met success criteria in 2 of 3 dry-run activations: consistent orientation change and no escalation; evidence manifests and SHA256 checksums present.
- Mobile Decoy (Prototype A) produced proof-of-concept attraction at short range but required additional ruggedization; scored medium on safety/feasibility.

Decision gate (assumed outcome):
- Proceed to Method 7: build a high-fidelity pulsed-light prototype with operationalized abort interlock and telemetry-grade evidence capture.
